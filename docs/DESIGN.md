# VarMQ docs design contract

Every page of the docs site follows this file. The values live in `src/styles/tokens.css`. The shared pieces live in `src/styles/ui.css` and `src/components/ui/`. The home page `/` (`src/components/Story.astro`, `src/styles/story.css`, and the sections under it in `src/components/home/`, `src/styles/home.css`) shows the system at its fullest.

## The idea

The queue is a roll of continuous-feed paper. A page is a print job. One square is one job. One lane is one worker.
A page explains by showing: each claim is a small working scene placed next to the Go call that makes it.

The site dresses that idea as signage for a job dispatcher, in the mascot's colours:

- **Colour.** Every green is the logo's (`assets/logo-light.svg` `#2e9c7f`, `logo-dark.svg` `#44bf9e`), deepened or lit; no colour comes from outside the mascot and the scenes. The logo green deepened (`--chrome`) is the structure: the header and the sidebar, with white lettering, edged in the bright logo green. The bright logo green (`--signal`) is the signal: the page you are on, the primary action, the highlighter stroke under a link. Amber (`--hot`), the colour every scene gives a job, numbers the sections, marks lists and is the caution tape. The scenes and the code are green-black panels in both themes.
- **Type.** Big Shoulders Display (`--headline`), condensed sign lettering in caps, for page titles, section heads and the home page's headings. IBM Plex Sans (`--display`) for reading and the interface. JetBrains Mono (`--mono`) for code, labels and the scenes.
- **Pattern.** A dot-matrix grid of logo-green dots behind every page, and the sprocket-hole rails on `/`: the paper of a dot-matrix printer.
- **Pieces.** The sidebar is a departure board: each page has its job number, and the page you are on is the lit green row. A page's banner is a ticket stub. Each h2 carries its section number (`§ 01`). A caution has hazard tape down its edge. Cards and buttons cast a hard green shadow and lift on hover. On `/`, a green ticker of job statuses runs between the story and the sections.

## Tokens

Use only the variables in `src/styles/tokens.css`. Never write a hex color in a page; the few exceptions are listed in "Adding a page or a component", step 4. The reader picks Light, Dark or System in the header's theme switch. System follows `prefers-color-scheme`. The tokens switch on `:root[data-theme='dark']`. Fumadocs' theme provider (next-themes, `components/fd/provider.tsx`) sets `data-theme` and the `dark` class before the first paint from the stored choice, so it carries from page to page.

## Type

- Body and headings use `--display` (Bricolage Grotesque). Code, labels, captions and numbers use `--mono` (JetBrains Mono). The shell `Head` loads both fonts once. A page stylesheet never imports them.
- Body: 1.0625rem, line height 1.6.
- H1 on the landing page: `clamp(2.375rem, 4.4vw, 3.75rem)`, weight 700, optical size 96, letter spacing -0.035em, line height 0.98, `text-wrap: balance`. Where it is short of height it steps down to 1.875rem: where the text's place (`.st-text`, a size container) is under 26rem tall, and, stacked before the tour (see The page), on a screen at most 830 px tall.
- A step's title on the landing page: `clamp(2rem, 3.2vw, 2.625rem)`, weight 700, optical size 72, letter spacing -0.03em, line height 1.05; 1.625rem where `.st-text` is under 25rem wide, 1.375rem where it is under 21rem tall. The section heads under the story: `clamp(1.875rem, 4.2vw, 3.25rem)`, weight 700, optical size 72, letter spacing -0.03em, line height 1.02 (`.h2`, `home.css`).
- Articles set their own heading sizes in `fd-theme.css`: `--local-h1` `clamp(2.25rem, 5.4vw, 3.75rem)` (the landing h1's maximum) and `--local-h2` `clamp(1.625rem, 2.8vw, 2.125rem)`, smaller than the landing page's h2s.
- H3: 1.25rem, weight 650.
- Lede on the landing page: `clamp(1.0625rem, 1.5vw, 1.25rem)`, line height 1.5, `--ink-2`, maximum 40ch. It is dropped where `.st-text` is under 20.6rem tall, and, stacked before the tour, on a screen at most 588 px tall.
- Small text on the landing page (a step's source link): `--st-small`, 0.9375rem.
- Labels: mono, uppercase. A scene's control label (`.slabel`) is 0.6875rem with letter spacing 0.1em; a banner line is 0.75rem with 0.08em (its `JOB nn` chip 0.1em); the sidebar's group labels are 0.75rem with 0.1em.

## Layout

- An article's column is Fumadocs' page column (up to 900 px) with its own padding. The landing frame is `--st-max` (1440px) with `--st-gutter` at each side: the `--gutter`, and at least 52px from 1180px up so it clears the sprocket-hole rails. No horizontal scroll from 375 px up.
- The page background is `--paper` under the dot grid (`--dot`, 20 px pitch). Lines are solid `--rule` hairlines, or `--chrome` where a line closes a page (the footer, the meta row). The dark panels (scenes, code) take `--panel-shadow`: a hard `--chrome` offset in light, a hairline in dark.
- Radius: 4 px for chips, kbd, lane tracks and a scene's number and text inputs; 8 px for scene buttons (`.pbtn`), selects, field boxes (`.sfield`), the tabs inside a tab bar, and cards; 10 px for a page's main-action buttons (`.btn`) and the against-VarMQ boxes; 12 px for code cards, segmented tabs, the install box, the theme switch, small maps and the Output panel; 16 px for scene frames and data panels.
- A scene measures its own width with `@container sframe`, not the viewport.

## Motion and interaction

- Use `--ease`. Keep each animation under 1.2 s, except a loop: a scene's loop has a Pause, and the home page's maps are the exceptions (below).
- Every looping animation in a scene has a visible pause button (`.pbtn`). On the home page the intro's map runs the whole network in a loop, with no Pause (the owner's choice; a known gap against WCAG 2.2.2, below), and rests while the hero is out of view or under the header. On the tour each step plays once. The small maps under the story have no Pause either (the owner's choice, the same gap): JOB 02's three scenes each loop on their own timeline while in view, and JOB 03's stage runs its jobs while in view; both rest out of view. Under reduced motion each scene shows one still frame that still shows its order, and the stage's buttons jump to their end state. JOB 04's lanes count up once, over 1.1 s with 60 ms between lanes, when 35% of a lane list first comes into view, and again on Replay; under reduced motion they show their values at once. The rails, an ornament, drift on their own, and a comet of `--brand` circles the tour button's border every 3 s (a conic gradient on an animated `@property` angle, masked to the border); neither has a Pause. The map, the rails and the comet stop under reduced motion. The first paint does not fade in: the hero and its map show at once. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`: show the end state and do not loop. A scene that plays steps offers a Step button.
- Interactive scenes work with the keyboard. Each control has a visible focus ring: 2 px, 3 px offset, `--accent-text` on paper and `--panel-accent` on a dark panel.
- A scene shows the real model. It does not draw a result that the library cannot produce.

## Content rules

- Do not invent features, numbers, customers or quotes. A number comes from the library source, the docs, or `github.com/goptics/benchmarks`.
- Show results that go against VarMQ next to the results that favor it.
- Memory figures are MB = 10^6 bytes on every page. `benchmarks.goptics.org` divides by 2^20, so its MB figure is 4.6 % lower. The Benchmarks page says so.
- Write for Go developers. Use short sentences and plain words. Keep API names in code format.
- Keep existing facts, headings and anchors of a docs page unless the change is the point of the task. Do not break inbound links.

## Accessibility

- Text contrast is AA in both themes. Do not use color alone to carry meaning.
- Each interactive scene has a text alternative that states what it shows (`describe`).
- Use real headings in order. Use landmarks. Keep the skip link.
- A touch target is at least 44 px on a coarse pointer. The `@media (pointer: coarse)` block at the end of `ui.css` sizes the shared pieces (`.pbtn`, tabs, fields, copy buttons, the theme switch). The one in `fd-theme.css` sizes the code blocks' Copy button. `home.css` sizes the code card's fold row, its head buttons, lone links and the benchmark lanes' library links; `bench.css` the explorer's selects, Show radios and Channel baselines toggle; `story.css` the tour's captions. A new control joins the list for its stylesheet.

## The home page story

`/` opens on the intro with the map, and offers varmq's story as a tour of ten steps (`components/Story.astro`, `lib/story/`, `styles/story.css`):

- **The page.** By default it is the hero, then the sections under it, and it scrolls as any page does: no snapping, nothing pinned. The hero has the text on the left and the map on the right; on portrait screens below 1024 px, and on any screen under 30rem wide, the two stack (below). Side by side, and on the tour, the hero is one screen under the header, pinned only on the tour, and its text column is a size container, so it sets itself by the room it has. Stacked before the tour, the text column sizes by its width only.
- **The intro.** The h1 and the lede (the hero fields of `index.mdx`). The h1 names two parts of the map in their colours as text: "Worker pools" in the goroutines' green (`--vm-go`) and "job queues" in the amber edge of its queues and jobs (`--vm-job-edge`), the map's own colours in each theme; the rest stays `--ink`. As large text its floor is 3:1: light 3.1:1 and 4.35:1, dark 8:1 and 9.8:1. Then the install box and Get started (the GitHub link is in the header, on phones too), and the whole map with the tour button on it. The map's still frame is in the HTML at first paint (`lib/heromap/still.ts`); once the animation's code has loaded, the map runs as in the last step, from where its camera has pulled back, holds 1.5 s once still, and runs again, until the reader takes the tour. It rests while the hero is out of view or under the header, and runs again from its start when it is back. Stacked, the intro comes first and the map under it, at the size it has on every step (sized to leave 22rem for the text); the tour moves the map on top, where each step reads it. Until the tour the hero is not pinned and is only as tall as the intro and the map, with the sections' own space above it and JOB 02's padding under it, so the gaps match those between sections. Under 830 px tall the h1 steps down (see Type) and the gaps close up; under 588 px tall the lede goes. A 390 × 664 phone keeps the lede.
- **The tour.** A `.pbtn--paper` button, "Take the tour · 10 steps" with a play mark, sits at the foot of the map, centred 10 px above its edge, so it is on the first screen wherever the map is. On a map under 17rem wide it reads "Take the tour". It gives the story's track its 27 stops (the intro and one per caption), pins the stage under the header, plays step 1, and moves focus to its title. Without JS it is hidden (`layouts/Base.astro`); the story as text stays for screen readers.
- **The steps.** Each scroll plays the next caption of one of ten steps: that stretch of its chapter, once, at the map's own pace. A scroll back up within a step plays its last caption backward. A step reached going down plays from its start with every ring empty, up to the caption it stops at; reached going up, the map plays backward from the later step to that caption's finished frame (1 s at most) and holds there, every ring full. Back up from step 1 the tour ends at the intro, and the map plays backward to the end of its run, then loops. Step 10 plays once and holds its last frame. The one code block morphs into the code behind that chapter and lights the lines each caption is about. The code block is a dark panel in both themes, highlighted at build time with the same theme as the doc code blocks (`ec.config.mjs`).
- **Captions.** A caption is a button. It scrolls to that caption's stop, or plays the caption again if the page is there. The caption playing is in `--accent-text`; the others, done ones too, are `--ink-3`. Its point is a ring in `--brand`, and a line runs from ring to ring with its ends under the rings' strokes, so the captions read as one track that fills as the step plays.
- **Snapping.** On the tour the page snaps one stop at a time above its last step. One wheel turn is one stop, and a free-spinning wheel keeps stepping. From step 10 down the page scrolls normally, with every input; on the way back up, a wheel turn or a key that would pass step 10 stops there, and the story takes over again.
- **Leaving the tour.** There is no Exit button. Skip, one round 48 px button fixed at the bottom centre of the screen (in the corner under the map when the screen is under 30rem tall), shows on steps 1 to 10. Its outline is ten segments, one per step, that fill in `--brand` as the steps play, and its name says it skips the rest of the tour. It ends the tour: the track gives up its stops, the map goes back to the intro's loop (resting while the hero is out of view), and the page jumps to JOB 02's heading, `#sched-title`, at the top of the screen, which takes focus. Esc does the same. A scroll on past step 10 into the sections ends the tour too, where the reader is: the page moves up by what the track gave up, so the sections in view stay put, and back up the page reaches the hero, not the story. Back up at the intro (stop 0), at the foot of a page too short to scroll a stop past step 10, or tabbing out into the sections, the tour ends the same way, where the reader is; focus left in the story moves to what is in view: the h1 at the top, past the end the first section heading wholly on screen, or nothing if none is. Tabbing out keeps focus where the Tab put it and brings it into view. Esc in the search dialog only closes the dialog. A reload mid-tour opens on the page without it, at its top, and the page keeps its scroll on later reloads. The tour can start again from the map.
- **The scrollbar.** From 1180px up, where the rails show, the home page hides it (`scrollbar-width: none` on `:root:is([data-home], :has(.st))`); narrower, the rails are hidden and the browser's scrollbar shows. The page still scrolls with the wheel, keys and touch, and the glow on the rails shows where the reader is.
- **The sprocket-hole rails.** From 1180 px up, a 36 px rail runs down each side of the window, fixed, the window's full height and over the header too, with a `--rule` hairline edge toward the page. The header's padding there is `--st-gutter` (52 px), so it clears them; the rails draw over the header (`--z-nav`). A rail has one hole every 48 px, in its middle: punched, a little darker than the paper, with a thin `--brand` rim. The holes feed as paper does: the left rail's down and the right rail's up, 8 px a second on their own and a third as far as the page has scrolled. They are an ornament, the one thing on the page that moves without the reader. A 168 px glow band of lit holes in `--brand` runs down both rails from the top of the page to the bottom. `Hero.astro` eases the holes' offset and the band's place toward the scroll position each frame, so they glide after a snap, and moves them by transform only. Below 1180 px there are no rails. Under reduced motion the holes stand still and unlit.
- **Reduced motion.** The tour is still offered. Each step shows its last frame.
- **Checks.** Check the hero and every step at the 18 viewport sizes in `home-stage-fit-testing`, both themes. Test the wheel inside the page, not through CDP events alone.

### The sections under the story

### The map's two palettes

The map keeps its own palette (`lib/heromap/map.css`), in both themes:

- **Dark.** The map's original palette, which is not to be changed: amber jobs, green goroutines, a violet event loop, red failures, SQLite blue and Redis red.
- **Light.** The same hues, made dark enough to read on `--paper`:
  - Every text colour on the paper, and the strokes of every hue but green, has an OKLCH lightness of about 0.49 to 0.56, close to the page's own on-paper colours (`--accent-text`, the `--tk-*` values). At equal lightness no hue looks heavier than another. The text colours have at least 4.9:1 against `--paper`. The amber edge (`#a26500`) has 4.35:1, enough for a stroke and for the h1's large text. Text on a fill (the job's number, the check) is darker.
  - The green of a worker, its goroutines and their work is the light logo's (`--brand`), as the dark map's is the dark logo's, so the map and the logo share one green. As a mark it needs 3:1, and has 3.1:1.
  - Fills use the same hues at a lightness of about 0.96.
  - The greys are green-tinted rather than a cool grey, so the map matches the paper around it: `--ink-3` for labels, lines and the program box, and `#cddbd4` for the dot grid.
  - A job keeps the dark map's amber (`#f2b036`) with a darker edge (`#a26500`), which gives the square its 3:1 against the paper.
  - The light palette uses tokens where the page has one: `--brand` for green marks, `--accent-text` for green text, `--tk-s` for the amber text, and `--ink-3` for labels and lines.
- **Where it lives.** The palette is set on `:root`, so the story's text beside the map uses the same red, SQLite blue and Redis red (`--vm-*-text`), and the small maps under the story (`.vm-mini`) draw in it.

## Adding a page or a component

1. Put a scene in `src/components/<area>/` and import `ui/Scene.astro`. Import your area stylesheet in the scene's frontmatter. Do not import `tokens.css` or fonts.
2. Need a button, tab, field, code card or highlighter? Use the shared one. Need a new shared piece? Add it to `ui.css` or `components/ui/`, then list it above.
3. A value that no token carries is a `--local-` variable inside your component. If two areas need it, promote it to `tokens.css`.
4. No hex, `rgb()` or `hsl()` outside `tokens.css`. The exceptions are the map's palette (`heromap/map.css`, see "The map's two palettes"), `#fff` for the text on the primary buttons' green (`ui.css`, `home.css`), and JOB 04's `--trade` (`home.css`, see Signal). The map's grid-fade mask stops (`heromap/render.ts`) are white, but a mask reads only its alpha, so they are not colours. The design detector (`impeccable detect src`) must report nothing.
5. Before you merge: `pnpm build`, `pnpm test`, then open the page at 1440 px and 390 px in light and dark. No console error, no horizontal scroll.

## Decisions and known gaps

- Theme: Light, Dark or System, from Fumadocs' switch; System follows `prefers-color-scheme`.
- The docs run on Fumadocs over Astro (`@fumadocs/astro`). Astro renders each page, so the scenes stay Astro components with their own scripts; Fumadocs' layout is a React island around it, and gets the page as a slot it never re-renders. Links do full page loads, so every scene's script runs on each page it is on.
- The Go call strip sits under the stage in every scene (it sat on top in two areas).
- The home page's intro map loops with no Pause button, by the owner's choice. That falls short of WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide) for readers who have not set reduced motion; a small Pause on the map frame would close it.
- JOB 02's three scenes loop, and JOB 03's stage keeps running jobs, with no Pause, by the owner's choice: the same gap against WCAG 2.2.2 for readers who have not set reduced motion. Both stop out of view. A Pause in each banner would close it.
- A selected tab is never green (see Segmented tabs); the darkened green is the primary buttons' (see Brand).
- The map on `/` (`src/lib/heromap/map.css`) keeps its own `--vm-*` palette (see "The map's two palettes"). None of the dark palette's hex values are tokens. Neither are the light palette's amber (`#f2b036`, `#a26500`, the job number's `#4c2e02`), violet, red, blue, pale fills, dot grid (`#cddbd4`) or check (`#095e46`).
- Scene chips and lanes (`.ljob`, `.llane`, `.ga-*`, `.gb-*`, `.ops-job`) are still local. They read tokens, but each area has its own markup.
- `GoPlay.astro` reads the tokens. Its buttons are `.pbtn`. The `go-play` element itself (Run, Edit, Reset, Open in Go Playground, an optional Copy of the whole program with its edits, and the output) is `src/lib/go-play.ts`. `GoPlay.astro` and the home page's `Examples.astro` give it their own markup and styles, and only `Examples.astro` adds the Copy button.
- The explorer on the Benchmarks page and the matrix on the Comparison page keep their own table markup.
- Some local values skip the `--local-` prefix: the rails' `--hole`, `--rim`, `--pitch` and `--band` on `.roll` and the story's own layout values in `story.css`, the primary buttons' `--primary` on `.pbtn--primary` and `.btn--primary` in `ui.css`, and JOB 04's `--trade` on `.race` in `home.css`. A text amber for light paper would belong in `tokens.css`. The band's fade mask writes `black`; a mask reads only its alpha, so it is not a colour.
- The home page has two h2 sizes: a step's title, and the section heads under the story (`.h2`), larger, as the print jobs' titles. The closing's heading uses the display face at `clamp(2.25rem, 6vw, 4.5rem)`.
